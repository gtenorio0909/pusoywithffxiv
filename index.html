Pusoy Table

Chinese poker for 2 to 4 players. Split 13 cards into 3, 5 and 5.

Welcome
How to play
  1. One person taps Host a table and shares the invite link. Everyone else opens the link, types their name and joins.
  2. When 2 to 4 players are seated, anyone at the table can deal.
  3. Everyone gets 13 cards. Split them into a Front of 3 cards, a Middle of 5 and a Back of 5.
  4. The Back must be at least as strong as the Middle, and the Middle at least as strong as the Front. The table won't let you lock in a hand that breaks this.
  5. Drag cards into a row. On a phone, press and hold a card for a moment, then drag. You can also tap cards to select them and press Place. Drop a card on a card in a full row to swap them. Auto-arrange suggests a strong split.
  6. When everyone locks in, all hands are revealed and scored.
  • Scoring: you play each opponent row by row. Win a row: +1. Lose a row: −1. Ties score 0.
  • Sweep: win all three rows against someone and you get 3 bonus points (6 total).
  • Front hands: only three of a kind, pairs and high cards count. Straights and flushes don't apply to 3 cards.
  • Hand order: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card. A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest straight.
  • The host's device runs the table. If the host closes the page, the game pauses until they come back.
